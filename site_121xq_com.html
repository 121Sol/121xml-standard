Architecture

Sovereign, self-describing, verifiable.

Local control

Public/private-key ownership; you choose where data lives. No mandatory cloud.

Any → any

Self-describing packets carry their own structure and semantics — any protocol decodes without external namespaces.

Content-addressed

Objects addressed by the hash of their canonical bytes — immutable, deduplicated, tamper-evident.

Under the hood, every object is a signed Merkle-DAG of facets — payload, shape, context, rules, relations, provenance, and optional 4-D space. The full standard is at 121xml.com.

Hardware/processor-level acceleration is a separate long-horizon track (121XMLSilicon) and is deliberately not part of the software runtime.
