## The Next Era of AI: Four Principles

### 1. **Intelligence ≠ AI**
Intelligence comes from multiple sources: human expertise, domain knowledge, machine reasoning, statistical analysis, deterministic rules. We combine them all.

### 2. **Execution > Capability**
A mediocre algorithm that reliably produces results beats a brilliant algorithm that sometimes fails. 121XQ is built for delivery certainty.

### 3. **Results > Features**
Count what matters: business outcomes, not model parameters. 121XQ measures success by ROI, not by model size.

### 4. **Transparency > Trust**
Complete visibility into decision pathways, data flows, and result verification. Users understand *why* results were delivered.
